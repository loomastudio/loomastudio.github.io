Our story

Built by a developer who loves making games

Looma Studio was founded by Rashmi Virajitha with one goal: make great games accessible. Over the years that has grown into a library of more than 200 complete game projects across Unity, Unreal Engine and Godot.

Each project is built to be used. The code is clean and organized, the assets are complete, and the setup is easy, so studios, publishers and solo creators can launch quickly or build something bigger on top.

We also work directly with clients on custom games, reskins and ports, bringing the same care to every project, big or small.
