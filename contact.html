FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I get when I buy a game?
The complete project: all source code, scenes, art, audio and documentation. You can open it in the listed engine version, edit it, and publish it under your own brand.
How does payment work?
Add games to your cart and send an order request. We reply with a secure payment link (PayPal, Fiverr or bank transfer). The files are delivered as soon as payment is confirmed.
Can I make an offer on a game?
Yes! Every game has a "Make an offer" button. Send your price and any changes you need, and we'll get back to you, usually within 24 hours.
Can you reskin or customize a game for me?
Absolutely. We can change the art, theme, levels and features, add ads and in-app purchases, and prepare store builds. Mention what you need in your order or offer.
Can I publish the game on Google Play, the App Store or Steam?
Yes. The projects are made to be published. We can also help with builds, store listings and setup if you want.
Do you offer support after purchase?
Yes. We help you open, set up and build the project. Extra changes after delivery can be arranged as custom work.
Which engine should I choose?
Unity is great for mobile and cross-platform games, Unreal for high-end PC and console visuals, and Godot for lightweight, royalty-free 2D and web games. Not sure? Ask us and we'll advise you.
